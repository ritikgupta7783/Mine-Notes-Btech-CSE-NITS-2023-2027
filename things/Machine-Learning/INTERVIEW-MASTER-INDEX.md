## Interview answer pattern
1. Define the concept simply.
2. Explain why it matters.
3. Use a tiny example or formula.
4. State one limitation or common mistake.
5. Connect it to another ML concept.

Do not claim a question came from a particular company's interview unless its source and date are documented.
