# Module 06 — Statistics and Probability

**Prerequisite:** basic arithmetic and the Python/NumPy foundations.  
**Goal:** describe data, reason about uncertainty and understand the statistical terms used in model evaluation.

## Lessons
1. [Mean, median, variance and spread](01-descriptive-statistics.md)
2. [Probability, conditional probability and Bayes](02-probability-and-bayes.md)
3. [Distributions, sampling and uncertainty](03-distributions-sampling-inference.md)

## Why this comes before algorithms
A model's training data is only a sample of the world. Statistics helps us summarise its distribution, recognise variability and avoid overclaiming from limited evidence. Probability is used by algorithms such as Naive Bayes and logistic regression, and in interpreting predicted probabilities.

## Checkpoint
- Calculate mean and median for a small list.
- Explain why outliers affect mean more than median.
- Distinguish variance from standard deviation.
- Calculate a simple conditional probability.
- Explain correlation vs causation and statistical vs practical significance.
