# Module 17 — ML Interview Preparation

**Prerequisites:** foundational modules and at least one practical project.
The goal is to explain what a technique does, why you chose it, how you validated it, and what could go wrong—not just to recite definitions.

## Sections
1. [Core questions with answers](01-core-ml-questions.md)
2. [Maths, metrics and numerical questions](02-math-and-metric-questions.md)
3. [Scenario-based questions](03-scenario-based-questions.md)
4. [Code-reading and debugging drills](04-code-and-debugging-questions.md)
5. [Project walkthrough checklist](05-project-discussion-template.md)

## Recommended answer format
**Definition → why it matters → example/formula → limitation → connection to the pipeline.**

Never claim a question came from a specific company's interview unless the source and date are recorded. These are representative preparation questions.
